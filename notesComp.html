    Computing

    Hey! This is the last intro of the 5. It was a good run...unless this is the first one you are seeing - then, welcome.
    Several articles here might be obvious to someone with a formal background in Computer Science. Since I am trained in Maths, however, I couldn't help trying to connect things back to Mathematics. Even though some of the articles, such as Numerical Methods, are fairly math-heavy, the others are more about the process of figuring out how something works and build it myself. For example, the article named Browser-Based Flow Simulation, is probably a fairly standard template for webpage design. But that is not how I wrote it for this webpage. I started with an actual vector field in Cartesian coordinates, and somehow, by the time I was done coding, there were no signs of vector fields anywhere!
    That was an unsetlling feeling - seeing the math vanish.
    Anyways, Happy reading.
