Quantitative Finance, Stochastic Calculus and Scientific Computing. I like to make Math a little less abstract and more visualization driven. See my GitHub to explore all the visual tools I have made for mathematics. This is my Portfolio with simulations, interactive models and small games that make probability and differential equations easier to see.

My MS thesis (IISER Kolkata, 2025–26) develops stochastic calculus from measure-theoretic foundations and applies it to the Black–Scholes PDE. Current work covers Time Series Analysis for quantitative finance, Advanced Numerical approximation of financial option pricing models and Markov chain Monte Carlo methods in Finance.
