    Differential Equations

    These are some of my most favourite notes, mostly because the geometric treatment of DEs are not done in the standard math books. DEs have been quite an experience for me. Over the course of 4 years, I went through "OH! I understand this now!" and "ohh.. I do not understand this. Again." more times than I would like to admit. ODE was one of the first topics I started turning into geometry heavy notes - handwritten but nevertheless, my first.
    Happy reading!

    bash ~ /Notes/DE/ODE
    Ordinary Differential Equations PDF
    Existence and uniqueness via Picard-Lindelöf, planar autonomous linear systems, phase portraits, invariant manifolds, and Lyapunov stability criteria.
    bash ~ /Notes/DE/ODE
    Partial Differential Equations PDF
    Method of characteristics for quasilinear equations, Riemann invariants, shock surfaces, parabolic boundary value problems, and fundamental solutions.

    Fourier Analysis & Transforms PDF
    Fourier series decompositions, orthogonal projections in \(L^2\), integral transforms, convolution algebra, and spectral solutions to the heat & wave operators.
