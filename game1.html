Short explainer: Once you switch to math mode, you pretty much see what is happening. Do it, in case you have not yet. The grey dots are the random sampling done on the square. Inside the shape you pick, called N_Omega, green ones are on the shaded region and red ones are in the unshaded region. To see if you have correctly done the shading we look at green/(red+green) to get the area (approximated). Increase the number of sample points to get better estimates. This is part of something called Monte Carlo simulation.
Read the detailed Math of Monte Carlo and code behind this game at Notes on Computing.
p.s. The target fraction is between 1% and 99%. Some of the world's best minds were consulted and we all concluded the same thing. 0% and 100% will be too easy. Thank you for your cooperation!
