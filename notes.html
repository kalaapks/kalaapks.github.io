Stochastic Analysis & Quantitative Models

  • > Stochastic Calculus & Itô's Lemma PDF
    Semimartingales, quadratic variation, Itô formula, and stochastic integration: $$d(f(t, X_t)) = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t}dt + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}dX_t + \frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}d\langle X\rangle_t$$

    Formulation of the Black-Scholes PDE, Cauchy-Euler transformations, and American option free boundaries.
