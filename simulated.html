Simulations & Visualizations

This is my absolute favourite part of the website.

This section is a quick glimpse into how I see Mathematics. There is immense beauty and power in abstracting ideas, but I have always enjoyed going in the other direction too — stripping away the abstraction and trying to see the geometry hiding underneath it. So, rather than explaining everything here, I will only show what pages and pages worth of theorem encodes. All of them are connected to ideas developed more carefully in the notes, so feel free to follow the links and see where the pictures came from.

These ranges from geometric ODE/PDE solutions and numerical approximations, computational models and stochastic trajectories that I generated for my finance notes.
