Simulations & Visualizations

This is my absolute favourite part of the website.

This section is a quick glimpse into how I see Mathematics. There is immense beauty and power in abstracting ideas, but I have always enjoyed peeling away the abstraction and seeing the geometry hiding underneath it, whenever possible. So, this section is essentially a collection of things that a 5 year old would say "Nice color-y things". If you are 5, you know exactly what I mean, right?

These are not meant to replace the mathematics. They are little windows into it. All of them are linked back to the corresponding Notes where they are developed more carefully, feel free to click and see where the pictures came from.
Happy viewing!
